## 2. What the record actually contains

The recruiter's notes fall under four headings, with a verdict per heading. Reconstructed wording is in brackets where the transcript mis-hears.

### Communication — "close, but narrated"

- Problem context articulated with clarity. Research deep and strategic. Briefing well structured.
- Design decisions not explicitly connected back to the problem just framed. The quoted example: *"we brought the design system into [Claude] Code"* stated without the why behind the choice.
- Challenged on **scope**, **visual inconsistencies**, and **unclear navigation**, the answer was *"that's not something I'm super happy with"* rather than a defence or an alternative.
- *"You showed it rather than convinced of it."*

### Craft — "visually very competent, details below the bar"

Three concrete defects, all on the **knowledge vault surface**:

| # | As read | Likely referent | Check that should have caught it |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | "colors in the legend that don't match, black versus green" | A legend swatch and the state it describes disagree | Tier 2 "a bar agrees with its own legend" (built after this, 2026-08-27) |
| C2 | "semantic copy that's inconsistent, three [integration] errors versus 700 [Drive]" | Two counts on the same surface in different grammar: a count of errors phrased one way, a count of documents from a Drive connector another | The **copy lexicon** proposed in `00-plan-revised.md` §4.3, not yet built |
| C3 | "unclear visual [hierarchy] where the selected state doesn't communicate with sufficient feed-forward" | A selected row or tab that does not look selected before you act on it | Tier 2 selected-state check proposed in §4.3, not yet built |

- The defects were acknowledged when pointed at but not anticipated: *"discovered during the interview."*
- The panel expected *"more proactivity towards the questions they asked you to explore deeper."*
- The [dock / bottom bar] is *"well thought out."*

### Problem solving — "exceptional"

- Context, the three-layer model (Enable / agency / individual) and why permissioning is critical from the ground up: all clear.
- But the solutions do not always connect explicitly back to the problem framed.
- **The daily brief** solves data reconciliation but is *"presented as different widgets, correct but impersonal."*

### Scope — "no alternative proposed"

- Mohamed asked whether a smaller MVP could have come first, and named one: **email-forwarding of commission opportunities.**
- The answer justified the approach taken, then said both could have run in parallel.
- The panel wanted the smaller scope to come from the candidate, earlier, unprompted.
- Constantin's rebuttal on the call: team resources made parallel work impossible, and that constraint was stated in the interview. The recruiter did not contest it and could not, not having been present. The panel's point stands regardless of the rebuttal: the smaller scope was not offered before it was asked for.

### Outcome

- "Very, very close." No other candidate was hired. Re-application possible after six months against a matching opening, so the earliest Deel window is **March 2027**.

## 3. What is new against the five-line reconstruction

The plan's tests T1 to T5 hold. Three things the reconstruction did not have:

1. **A defect list exists.** C1 to C3 above seed §4.3 directly, and one of the three (C1) is already covered by an existing tier-2 check. Assumption A2 is retired.
2. **The AI-authorship line.** Constantin's own opening reflection: the case study showed *how to operate with AI* under time constraints, but did not confirm *that the human is in charge of every design decision*, and the panel's quoted example of narration is precisely a sentence about the tool. This is not a sixth test. It sharpens T1: every decision is stated as Constantin's, with the evidence it rests on; the tooling appears only under *Enforced by* and in the method, never as the source of a rationale. The sentence "we brought the design system into Claude Code" is replaced by the reason the system had to be executable at all, which the eval README already states.
3. **The smaller scope has a name.** Email-forwarding of commission opportunities is the counter-example the panel supplied. The T5 narrative states it, states the resource constraint that ruled out parallel delivery, and states what was learned from choosing the larger scope, all before anyone asks. The decision-log field *Alternatives considered* on the brief and on commissions cites it by name.

Two smaller additions to the defence rehearsal (§4.7): **navigation** is a named challenge, so the dock needs its three challenges and counter-proposals written down; and "proactivity towards the questions asked" means the rehearsal should include the follow-up question a panellist asks after the first answer, not only the first.
