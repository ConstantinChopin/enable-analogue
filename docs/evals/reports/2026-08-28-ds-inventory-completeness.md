## 2. Over-inventory / wrong grouping

- **EyeOff "gated-absent" icon** — in tension with the product's own absent-not-masked rule (GateMark, E5, F9): gated content renders *nothing*. Its only sanctioned use is the optional admin-only note; as listed, it invites building masked placeholders the specs forbid. Either scope the entry to "admin-only note marker" or drop it.
- **TrendingUp (projection)** — the timeline renders projected/due/paid as words with provenance per DEC-22 and entity-display's words-not-color rule; no surface visibly demands the icon. Weak demand; harmless.
- **Columns2 / Download** — fine to keep, but both flows are §6-excluded (schematic); worth annotating so nobody builds Compare on build day because the icon exists.
- Everything else in the inventory traces to a real demand source. The cite-your-demand method held.

## 3. Verdict

The inventory is **strong and unusually honest** — the demand-citation discipline caught almost everything, the trust-state set and the queue/composer organisms are complete, and §6's exclusions are mostly the right ones. The misses cluster in three bands: (1) two **scope holes** the build will hit hard — the commission calendar (a named Journey C screen with zero inventory presence) and the 390px contradiction between PhoneStack's "beat 8 only" and five journeys' acceptance criteria; (2) a band of **named-but-homeless states and blocks** (copy/export split, departure checklist, dual-currency money, ended-pending-close, histories, trip history, amenity distinction, second-advisor account) that are each cheap alone but expensive as day-of surprises because most are acceptance criteria or demo-visited; (3) small-bore icon/enum/state gaps that a component-by-component build pass will absorb. One entry (EyeOff) actively contradicts a stated design rule and should be re-scoped. Fix #1 and #2 by decision (build it schematic, or write the exclusion), fold #3–#12 into the inventory before the build starts, and the inventory is build-ready.

**Counts:** 2 High · 10 Medium · 8 Low · 3 over-inventory notes.
