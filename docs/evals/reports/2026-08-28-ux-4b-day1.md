# Eval 4b — UX quality, Day 1 (2026-08-28)

**Target:** running prototype at localhost:3000. Routes driven: /briefing, /commissions/vo, /records, /records/maison-leandre, /ask, /ask?state=refusal, /travellers/s-marchetti, /admin/review/sereno, /knowledge, /notices.
**Method:** accessibility-tree reads + computed-style probes (contrast ratios computed from resolved rgb over effective background; `font-variant-numeric` read per element; real keyboard Tab through the resolve sheet), live interaction tests on every actionable trust surface, viewport emulation at 375 and 390 px.
**Policy honoured:** design-system-inventory Rev 2 declared cuts (schematic Compare/Export, agenda-list calendar, light-only theme, stubbed ⌘K) are not findings. The 390 px scope *as declared* — named transforms on demo screens, no-horizontal-scroll everywhere else — **is** the standard the build was judged against, and it fails it (F-1).
**Class guideline citations:** ui-ux-pro-max UX database — Accessibility/Color Contrast ("minimum 4.5:1 for normal text"), Interaction/Focus States, Accessibility/Color Only, Responsive/Table Handling ("no wide content breaking layout"), Forms/Visible labels, Accessibility/Heading Hierarchy.

---

## Verdict

The trust surfaces — the product's thesis — are genuinely well built. Conflict, refusal, held-value, and provenance all read as load-bearing: the resolve sheet pairs each source with its date, standing ("Signed terms" / "Superseded rate" / "Uncorroborated"), a corroboration count, and an ImpactPanel ("one decision, three places"); the refusal renders calm and neutral (bg `#F8F8F9`, not red) with a form+color contract checklist and the held sources visible-but-excluded; the admin candidate holds a converted figure without source currency and says why. Status visibility is exemplary ("Synced 12:04", "booking-system figures up to 48h behind", per-document sync cadence, "8 of 128 shown"). Reduced-motion is globally honoured, money is verifiably `tabular-nums`, dialog focus management is correct.

What fails is the shell around that craft: **the 390 px layer does not exist on any route**, three visible affordances on demo-checkpoint screens are **inert**, and the soft status chips miss the contrast floor.

## Findings

### Demo-risk (3)

**F-1 [demo-risk][class-practice] — No 390 px behaviour anywhere; every route horizontally scrolls.**
Evidence: at emulated 390×844 (and 375×812 with mobile UA), `document.documentElement.scrollWidth = 770` vs `clientWidth = 390` on **all ten routes**; the 232 px sidebar keeps `display: flex`. Rev 2's own declared cut promises full PhoneStack transforms on briefing/ask/records/traveller and a no-horizontal-scroll floor everywhere else — currently neither tier is met on any screen. (ui-ux-pro-max: Responsive/Table Handling, Layout "no horizontal scroll".) If the demo ever leaves the laptop — projector at odd resolution, a phone handed across the table — the app visibly breaks.

**F-2 [demo-risk] — All three refusal recovery actions are inert.**
Evidence: on /ask?state=refusal, clicking "Ask the rep firm", "Show me the two sources", and "Flag for review" produces zero DOM change (`body.innerHTML` length 39,747 before and after; no banner, no highlight, no navigation). The refusal is presenter checkpoint 5; these buttons are the "refusal is not a dead end" argument, and right now they are the dead end. Either wire them (highlight the rail sources; ConfirmBanner "flagged for review"; prefill the follow-up) or remove/`schematic`-badge them.

**F-3 [demo-risk][consistency] — Traveller profile tab strip is decorative.**
Evidence: on /travellers/s-marchetti, "Overview / Journeys / Intelligence / Communications / Financials" are leaf `<span>`s in a `<div>` — no button/link ancestor, no `role`, `cursor: auto`. On /records and /knowledge the same visual pattern (TabSet) is real buttons. An interviewer who clicks "Financials" gets nothing, on the screen that carries checkpoint 7. Make them switch (even to schematic panels) or restyle so they don't read as tabs.

### Accessibility (6)

**F-4 [a11y] — Soft status chips fail the 4.5:1 contrast floor.**
Computed on /briefing at 11.5 px / 500 weight: warn chip "1 overdue" `#B07A10` on `#FDF4E0` = **3.40:1**; ok chip "paid 28 Jul" `#1A8A4A` on `#E8F5EE` = **3.92:1**. These are the highest-stakes glanceable tokens in the product (overdue money). Darken the on-soft text tokens (e.g. warn text toward `#8A5D06` ≈ 4.6:1, ok text toward `#177444`). (ui-ux-pro-max: Accessibility/Color Contrast, severity High.)

**F-5 [a11y] — Muted-on-muted combinations sit at 4.49:1 (just under the line).**
`#6F7076` on `#F4F4F5` — nav count badges (312 / 1,284), tab counts, applied filter chips ("Paris", "Programme: Atelier"), the "Info" severity chip, and the "schematic" tag (10 px). One token nudge (`#6A6B71` or darker) clears every instance at once.

**F-6 [a11y] — Share-tier radios have no accessible name.**
In the Sharing sheet, the three `role="radio"` buttons (`aria-checked` correct) expose **no name** — tier labels ("Collaborator — Full" etc.) are sibling text, not associated. A screen reader hears "radio, checked" three times on the surface whose whole point is who-sees-what. Add `aria-labelledby` to each.

**F-7 [a11y] — Records category tabs lack tab semantics; selection is background-only.**
"Hotels 209 / Cruises / DMCs / Rep firms": no `role="tab"`/`aria-selected` (nor `aria-pressed`), active state encoded only as `background-color` `#F4F4F5` vs transparent — the one state in the app conveyed by color alone. (ui-ux-pro-max: Accessibility/Color Only.)

**F-8 [a11y] — Heading level skip on /briefing.**
H1 ("Good morning…") jumps to H3 widget headings with no H2. Same pattern likely elsewhere. (ui-ux-pro-max: Accessibility/Heading Hierarchy, Medium.)

**F-9 [a11y] — Narration toggle is icon-only with `title` but no `aria-label`.**
The presenter-bar icon button (Presentation icon) exposes an empty accessible name in some AT combinations; every other icon-bearing control in the app carries text. Add `aria-label="Narration overlay"`.

### Class practice (2)

**F-10 [class-practice] — QuickLookRail cannot be dismissed.**
On /records the rail has no close control, document-level Escape does nothing (synthetic keydown verified — unlike the Radix sheets, which do close), and re-clicking the selected row does not deselect. Non-modal and place-preserving is right for the class; un-dismissable is not — "user control: close/escape everywhere" is a 4b heuristic. Add an X + Escape handling (the master-detail rule already reserves "Open full ↦" as the forward path).

**F-11 [class-practice] — Ask composer has no visible send affordance.**
The follow-up form contains only the text input (`aria-label` present; submits on Enter). Daily-use conversational surfaces ship an explicit send button — it is also the only submit path visible on touch. Minor.

### Consistency (2)

**F-12 [consistency] — Citation numbering appears only in the refusal state.**
Refusal rail sources are numbered (1, 2); the answered state's Sources panel is unnumbered and the answer body carries no CitationMark binding claims to sources — entity-display specifies "numbered citation" as the source chip form. The 12% claim and the p.4 extract line up today only because there is one claim.

**F-13 [consistency] — One inert chrome pattern among three real ones** (same evidence as F-3, logged here so the pattern rule is on record: every element styled as the TabSet must be interactive or visibly schematic).

Trivial: the "+3%" incentive chip on /briefing computes `font-variant-numeric: normal` while every other numeric surface computes `tabular-nums`.

## What passed (evidence on file)

- **Status visibility:** persistent "Synced 12:04" footer; "booking-system figures up to 48h behind" on both money surfaces; per-doc "12:04 · every 15 min"; "drive · live / 3 intranet errors" health chips; honest truncation labels ("8 of 128 shown").
- **Recognition over recall:** provenance popover in place on every field (What/Where/When + permission-checked "open document"); FreshnessDate as words ("96d unverified"); attribution on every preference and note.
- **User control / error prevention:** reminder is draft-first ("Drafted for your review — edit freely", labelled textarea, Send/Discard) and Send writes an attributed chase-log entry; notice close demands a required reason and states consequences; "Proceed knowingly (recorded)"; "Reject… (reason logged)"; Escape closes both Radix sheets and dialogs with focus returned.
- **A11y floor (where built):** body text passes 4.5:1 everywhere outside F-4/F-5; global `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--ring) }` plus shadcn ring classes; real-Tab through the resolve sheet shows a trapped, visibly-outlined focus path (solid 1.5px `rgb(28,29,34)`); `prefers-reduced-motion` kills all transitions/animations globally; form inputs labelled (reminder, close-reason, follow-up).
- **Data-density ergonomics:** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` computed on all money and counts; EvidenceDot = colored dot + full-contrast words (never color alone); severity chips carry words everywhere; queues are rows, directories are cards + rail, money is rows — per entity-display.
- **Trust-surface craft:** conflict, refusal, held, and provenance surfaces all carry more visual weight than commercial states; refusal is calm neutral, not error-red; held values state their exclusion in words on /ask, /records/maison-leandre, and /admin/review/sereno alike.
- **Alert restraint:** one Critical banner on briefing, chips elsewhere; no toast noise; ended incentive "stops feeding projections" stated in place.
